DailyCount Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

DailyCount ("the App") is a daily counting, activity tracking, reminder, and expense-recording application.

Information Stored in the App

DailyCount may store information that you enter into the App, such as daily activity records, counts, task names, reminder settings, dates, and expense records. This information is used to provide the App's counting, history, reminder, and expense features.

Where Your App Data Is Stored

DailyCount is designed to store your daily records and settings locally on your device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise. The developer does not receive your personal activity or expense records through the App's normal local-recording functionality.

Notifications and Alarms

If you enable reminders, DailyCount may schedule notifications or alarms on your device. Notification permission may be requested where required by Android. You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

Advertising

DailyCount uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information about ad interactions as described in their applicable policies. Advertising data handling is controlled by Google and its advertising partners.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App and its data handling secure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

DailyCount is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns about DailyCount, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the DailyCount Google Play Store listing.

Your Choices

You can manage notification permissions and advertising-related settings through Android and Google settings available on your device. You can also stop using the App and uninstall it at any time.
